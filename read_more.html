As the crisp Scandinavian autumn breeze sweeps across Gamla Stan, STOCK.HOLM proudly presents its most anticipated outerwear capsule: the Autumn-Winter Nordic Collection. Designed to embrace both harsh Northern climates and contemporary street refinement, this new lineup encapsulates clean architectural cuts, tactile organic warmth, and silent luxury.

Each silhouette in this release pays homage to Stockholm’s legendary urban aesthetic—where utilitarian functionality seamlessly blends with effortless sophistication. From hand-brushed heavyweight wool trench coats to ribbed oversized turtlenecks, every garment has been meticulously constructed using 100% eco-friendly, locally sourced Nordic fibers.

"We captured the quiet stillness of dusk in Stockholm—raw stone hues, structural warmth, and understated elegance."
— Astrid Lindqvist, Lead Designer

Key focus was placed on textile density and weight distribution, enabling full protection against cold winds without imposing cumbersome bulk. The color palette mirrors the seasonal transition of Swedish landscapes: deep volcanic slate, muted oat linen, dried terracotta, and icy moss green.

Whether layered under structured overcoats or worn as standalone statements, these high-gauge knitwear pieces promise unyielding durability and timeless charm for seasons to come.
