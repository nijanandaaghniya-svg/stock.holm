Sustainable Fabrics: Our Commitment

We are stepping forward into eco-friendly manufacturing by utilizing 100% organic linen and recycled wool for all upcoming apparel lines.

Source: Unsplash

As part of our continuous commitment to environmental stewardship, STOCK.HOLM is proud to announce a pivotal shift in our material sourcing strategy. Moving forward, all new outerwear and apparel lines will incorporate exclusively sustainable materials.

100% Organic Linen

Sourced directly from certified European farmers using zero synthetic pesticides and minimal water footprint.

Linen remains one of the most durable and biodegradable textiles available. By shifting entirely to organic farming practices, we reduce soil degradation while maintaining the crisp, breathable quality our garments are known for.

Recycled Wool Program

Repurposing high-gauge post-consumer wool garments to create luxury-grade winter overcoats.

Through closed-loop mechanical recycling, collected wool waste is sorted, shredded, and re-spun into dense virgin-quality yarns—eliminating the need for intensive land grazing and chemical dyeing processes.
