STOCK.HOLM Concludes Landmark Experiential Pop-Up in Jakarta

“Over 45,000 visitors immersed themselves in modern Scandinavian minimalism over three weeks. Here is what comes next for the flagship fashion label.”

By Astrid Lindqvist Senior Fashion & Culture Correspondent
Read Time: 4 Min |
FONT:
Exclusive Event Coverage — SCBD, Jakarta

The architectural interior of the STOCK.HOLM Jakarta pop-up fused monolithic concrete textures with warm Nordic birchwood.

Photo: STOCK.HOLM Media Press Kit

JAKARTA — Following three unprecedented weeks of queues spanning the South Central Business District, Swedish luxury minimalist label STOCK.HOLM has officially concluded its first experiential retail pop-up in Indonesia. The brand’s debut event proved to be an overwhelming triumph, drawing collectors, stylists, and fashion enthusiasts from across Southeast Asia.

"The energy and appreciation for clean design in Jakarta exceeded our highest expectations. This pop-up was not just a store; it was a conversation between Stockholm's heritage and Indonesia's vibrant creative spirit."

— Gustav Lindström, Creative Director at STOCK.HOLM

A Celebration of Minimalist Craftsmanship & Outerwear

The temporary space showcased an exclusive curated range of the label's Autumn/Winter architectural outerwear, lightweight technical tailoring designed specifically for tropical urban climates, and handcrafted leather accessories. Visitors experienced interactive audio-visual installations detailing the sustainable lifecycle of the garments—from organic Scandinavian wool sourcing to zero-waste pattern drafting.

What's Next: Permanent Flagship Boutiques Coming Soon

Due to the overwhelming demand and high turnout during the pop-up season, STOCK.HOLM executive leadership has officially announced plans for **two permanent flagship boutiques** in Indonesia set to open by late 2027.

While locations remain under wraps, hints suggest the primary boutique will reside in central Jakarta’s luxury retail corridor, with a secondary flagship planned for Bali’s cultural center in Seminyak.
